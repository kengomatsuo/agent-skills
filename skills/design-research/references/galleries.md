# Gallery sources for the Galleries agent

Add a gallery here only after opening it and reading what it says about itself. Counts drift, so re-read the page before quoting one.

## Kombai Gallery (added 2026-11-03)

[kombai.com/gallery/web/](https://kombai.com/gallery/web/), with a Mobile tab beside Web. Read on 2026-11-03 at laptop width; the Mobile tab was not browsed, and nothing was tried at phone width.

- **What it holds.** The page says 20,000+ designs, 13,570 of them web, sorted by category: dashboard 383, hero 370, navbar 299, footer 251, FAQ 199, forms 198, card 171, carousel 111, landing pages 140, chat 50, and more. Its FAQ says mobile has complete screens, product flows, sections and components.
- **Cost and terms.** "Forever free" for people and agents, per its FAQ. The terms page was not read, so do not copy code or assets from it.
- **Origin of the designs.** The page does not say who made them. The brands are invented (Oriel, Hollis, Tidewell), so treat a design as a layout idea and never as proof that a real product shipped it. Pair each one with a real product from the Real products agent.
- **Use.** The Galleries agent searches the category that matches the screen (dashboard, forms, sign in, pricing) and saves a screenshot with its URL. The "copy a reference" button hands a prompt to a coding agent; do not use it, since the brief decides what each agent does.
